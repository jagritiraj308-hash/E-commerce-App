Step 01 · Research & structure

From intent
to ownership.

Before pixels, I mapped what a shopper actually needs at each moment: enough confidence to choose, enough control to adjust, and no surprises at the check-out. These journeys and wireframes are the blueprint for the interactive prototype.

01

Research snapshot

A focused, assumption-light brief based on common e-commerce friction points and a primary shopper profile.

AK

Ananya — the considered buyer

28 · Designer · Jaipur · Shops on mobile after work

“I know roughly what I want. Help me compare quickly, then let me check out without hunting for the details.”
GoalFind a few well-made pieces for a new home.
BehaviourSaves first, buys after a second visit.
ConstraintLimited time; cautious about delivery and returns.

Friction → design response

Too many near-identical products→Five clear categories, chips, sort and a focused filter sheet.
Costs revealed too late→Delivery threshold and running total visible in the bag and both checkout steps.
Uncertain commitment→Save for later, reorder from history, and a low-risk 14-day return note.
Form fatigue→Prefilled demo address, inline validation and card/UPI choice.
